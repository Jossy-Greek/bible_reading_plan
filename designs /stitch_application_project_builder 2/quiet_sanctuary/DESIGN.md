## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a serene, contemplative, and tactile mobile experience tailored for meditative reading and personal spiritual discipline. Rooted in Material 3 principles, the aesthetic evokes illuminated manuscripts, high-grade bookbindery, and monastic quietude through a clean modern lens.

The target audience seeks a sanctuary away from the frenetic cadence of social media, engagement loops, and hyper-gamified habit trackers. Emotional resonance centers around reassurance, quiet accomplishment, peace, and internal motivation.

### Core Visual Tenets
- **Parchment & Ink**: The screen replaces harsh synthetic whites with organic, warm, light-refracting parchment surfaces. Text mimics deep-settled calligraphic ink rather than cold digital blacks.
- **Generous Sanctuary Whitespace**: Information density remains intentionally restrained. Screens never feel crowded; each viewport focuses attention on one primary purpose or passage.
- **Flat Elevation, Tactile Precision**: Surfaces rely strictly on chromatic depth, borderless white planar surfaces, and soft radii rather than drop shadows or heavy skeuomorphism.
- **Dignified Typography**: Tabular, light-weighted numerals sit alongside humanist sans-serif structures to preserve tranquility during active timed sessions.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural earth pigments: iron gall ink, ancient vellum, deep river clay, and hammered leaf.

### Functional Roles
- **Parchment Background (`#FBF8F1`)**: The canvas foundation for all default screens. Evokes aged cotton rag paper; soft on the eyes during dawn and late-night reading.
- **Parchment Deep (`#F1EBDD`)**: Utilized for track backgrounds, segmented control troughs, empty heat-map cells, locked badge canvases, and inactive progress rings.
- **Teal Primary (`#2F5D62`)**: The definitive action hue. Applied to primary call-to-action buttons, active rings, active countdown indicators, and prominent progress states.
- **Ink Primary (`#1F3A3D`)**: The primary text color. A deep, desaturated blue-slate ink that ensures high legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Ink Soft (`#52696B`)**: Secondary typography, scripture citations, inactive iconography, and auxiliary metadata.
- **Gold Accent (`#C6A15B`)**: Used sparingly for streaks, special milestone crowns, and reverent highlights.
- **Success Green (`#4E7D5B`)**: Marks completed chapters, verified sessions, full-meter reading bars, and triumphant affirmations.
- **Missed Red (`#C77B6A`)**: A soft terracotta red utilized for skipped calendar days, missed dates, and destructive reset actions.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes quiet clarity, balanced proportions, and high readability across phone viewports. `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers a gentle geometric presence with rounded warmth that aligns with sacred and reflective utility.

### Special Treatments
- **Tabular Figures**: Every numeric display—especially timers, countdown clocks, chapter numbers, and day counts—must mandate font feature settings `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter as digits cycle.
- **Reading Countdown**: Rendered extra-large at light weight (`font-weight: 300`) to evoke calmness, rather than anxiety or urgency.
- **Scripture Quotes**: Displayed in `body-md` italic styling, tinted in Ink Soft (`#52696B`), centered with relaxed line heights (`26px`) to invite lingering meditation.
- **Overline Metadata**: Group headers and uppercase category badges use `label-sm` with slight positive tracking (`+0.05em`) and uppercase transformation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid mobile-first experiences governed by a vertical rhythm of generous breathing room.

### Spatial Dimensions
- **Canvas Margins**: Default horizontal boundary is `1.25rem` (20px), increasing to `1.5rem` (24px) on wider mobile screens and small tablets.
- **Card Padding**: Every card container employs a uniform interior padding of `1.5rem` (24px) with zero external margins inside structured card groups.
- **Component Stacking**: Vertical separation between distinct content groupings (e.g., greeting to today's reading card) is strictly `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (32px), eliminating visual clutter.
- **Grid Layouts**: Badges and statistical overviews follow a balanced 2-column or 2x3 uniform grid with an 8px (`space-sm`) to 12px gap between tiles.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system intentionally rejects skeuomorphic drop shadows and ambient blur elevations. The depth model is strictly **tonal and planar**:

- **Card Surfaces**: Pure opaque white (`#FFFFFF`) floating directly on the parchment canvas (`#FBF8F1`). Cards use 0px blur and no drop shadow (`box-shadow: none`), relying entirely on the boundary contrast between white and `#FBF8F1`.
- **Secondary Surfaces**: Tonal wells such as segmented track housings, locked achievement cards, and inactive calendar states are set directly in Parchment Deep (`#F1EBDD`).
- **Over-the-Tabs Modal Layers**: Full-screen modal modes (Reading Session, Completion) take over the viewport completely with smooth alpha transitions, presenting a unified distraction-free surface.
- **Sheets**: Bottom sheets emerge with a subtle 1px border (`#F1EBDD`) around their perimeter top, anchored by a neutral grab handle.

## Shapes

The visual geometry conveys warmth, softness, and organic touch through sweeping corner radiuses:

- **Cards**: All primary cards have a consistent `20px` border radius (`1.25rem`).
- **Primary Buttons & Text Fields**: 56px interactive controls feature a matching `16px` radius (`1rem`), harmonizing with the card contours.
- **Pills & Status Chips**: Filter chips, weekday dots, and streak pills utilize fully rounded caps (`pill-shaped` / `9999px`).
- **Calendar & Badge Tiles**: Day cells within the monthly view and square badge housings carry a softened `12px` radius.
- **Progress Track Caps**: All line and ring progress terminals employ smooth rounded stroke caps (`stroke-linecap: round`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Full width, exactly 56px tall with a 16px corner radius. Solid Teal (`#2F5D62`) fill with pure white label text set in 17px semibold (`label-lg`). Inactive states (such as active reading timers before zero) stay at 100% opacity with dynamic countdown text in white, disabling pointer events until zero is reached.
- **Destructive Button**: Full width, 56px tall with Missed Red (`#C77B6A`) fill and white text. Used for irreversible resets in confirmation dialogs.
- **Text & Ghost Button**: Zero fill, minimum 48px touch target, colored with Teal (`#2F5D62`) or Ink Soft (`#52696B`).

### Chips (Choice & Filter)
- **Default State**: Fills with Parchment Deep (`#F1EBDD`), with Ink Primary text and no border. 36px height, rounded-full.
- **Selected State**: Fills with Teal (`#2F5D62`) with crisp white typography.
- **Streak Chip**: Sits at the top right of views. Features an emoji fire (`🔥`), Parchment Deep fill, Ink Primary semibold numeral, and 12px vertical padding.

### Cards
- **Base Card**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) background, 20px radius, 0px shadow, 24px inner padding.
- **Badge Tile (Locked)**: Parchment Deep fill at 60% opacity, 12px radius, 16px padding. Emoji icons rendered at 35% opacity with Ink Soft title text.
- **Badge Tile (Earned)**: Pure white fill, 12px radius, full chromatic emoji saturation, Ink Primary title, and Success Green completion label.

### Input Fields
- **Text & Number Inputs**: White fill (`#FFFFFF`), 16px radius, no border, 56px height, 16px horizontal padding. Placeholder text styled in Ink Soft (`#52696B`). Focused state highlights with a delicate 1.5px ring in Teal (`#2F5D62`).

### Bottom Navigation
- Fixed 5-destination bar at screen bottom: Home, Calendar, Progress, Badges, Settings.
- Container background is pure Parchment (`#FBF8F1`) with a hairline divider (`#F1EBDD`) along the upper edge.
- Destinations utilize dual-state icons (outlined when idle in Ink Soft; filled when active in Teal). Text labels use `label-sm` with active items colored in Teal.

### Progress Indicators
- **Ring Metric**: 96px diameter circular ring with a 9px stroke width, rounded caps, running Teal progress over a Parchment Deep background channel. Tabular percent figure centered within.
- **Linear Bar**: 6px height with fully rounded terminals (`9999px`). The empty rail is Parchment Deep (`#F1EBDD`); the filled status transitions dynamically from Teal to Success Green upon reaching 100%.

### Calendar Heatmap & Month Grid
- **Month Grid**: 7-column layout with 6px gaps. Days completed receive Success Green fill with a white check (`✓`). Missed days use an 18% Missed Red tint with a red numeral and hollow circle (`○`). The current day receives a 2px Teal stroke ring on a Parchment Deep base.
- **Year Heatmap**: 14px square cells with 3px gaps. Completed days are expressed in four discrete tonal densities of Success Green. Missed cells render in Missed Red at 35% alpha.
